Film Simulations - live comparison

After many years shooting with Canon, I switched to Fuji, but I’ve always struggled to clearly understand the differences between the various film simulations.
The in-camera descriptions are helpful, and the preview available here is interesting, but the images are small and the differences are not easy to interpret.

So I decided to build this small tool in the hope of helping those who, like me, are just getting started with Fuji film simulations.

Select a photo, BASE and COMPARISON. Drag to compare or switch to split view.
